# TMYK — The More You Know

TMYK is a party game about how well you know your friends. Choose Classic for four players or 8-Player Full Experience for five to eight players. Players write anonymous descriptions about one another, then try to figure out who wrote each one and who it describes.

## How to play

### 1. Choose a mode and gather your players

The host selects a mode when creating a room and shares its invite code. Classic needs exactly four players; 8-Player Full Experience needs five to eight. Once everyone in the room is ready, the host can start.

### 2. Write descriptions

Each player writes one short, kind description about each other player: three in Classic, or one for each of the other players in 8-Player Full Experience. Keep each description anonymous; each can be up to 250 characters. Once everyone submits, all descriptions are shuffled.

### 3. Guess who’s who

For each description, every player other than its author tries to guess:

- **Who wrote it?**
- **Who is it about?**

The writer sits that round out. Guesses are sealed until everyone eligible has locked in a guess. In Classic, the host chooses a timer for each description in the lobby: 30 seconds, 1 minute, 1 minute 30 seconds, or 2 minutes. 8-Player Full Experience always uses a server-controlled, fixed 30-second timer. If time runs out, unanswered guesses score zero; submitted guesses are kept.

### 4. Reveal and score

After the guesses are in—or time expires—the description and guesses are revealed. Correctly identifying the **author** earns 8 points; correctly identifying the **subject** earns 5 points. These are scored independently, so you can earn points for either or both.

The host advances the game to the next description. Classic has 12 descriptions. Full Experience has N × (N − 1) descriptions, where N is the number of players who started the match (20 to 56 rounds). After the final description, the player or players with the highest score win.

## Tiebreaker

If the top score is tied, the host can accept a shared win or start an optional author-identification tiebreaker. Each player tied for first writes one anonymous description about every other player in the room, for T × (N − 1) descriptions total. The descriptions are shuffled and shown one at a time with the subject visible. All players except the writer guess who wrote each description; the only author choices are the original tied players, and players cannot select themselves. Each correct guess earns one separate tiebreaker point; incorrect and unanswered guesses earn zero. Tiebreaker points never change main-game scores. After all descriptions, only the original tied players are ranked by these separate points; the leader wins, and players still tied share the win.

## A few helpful things to know

- Your descriptions and guesses are private until the game reveals them.
- Locked guesses cannot be changed.
- A disconnect does not count as a wrong guess. The game waits for all required guesses or for the round timer to expire.
- Be playful, be thoughtful, and keep it kind.
